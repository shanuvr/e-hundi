Problem:
We need the user's UPI app to open directly with the selected amount prefilled, but without a payment gateway we cannot verify if payment succeeded, causing the confetti to show even if the user cancels.

Solution:
Integrate PhonePe Payment Gateway using their UPI Intent API.

Why this method:
1. Opens the user's default UPI app instantly with the amount prefilled.
2. Charges 0% commission on standard UPI transactions (no money lost).
3. Reliably verifies payment success on the backend before triggering the confetti screen.
